Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Sungjun Lee (“we”, “us”), the developer of Nodii for macOS and iOS (the “App”) and its related online service (together, the “Service”), handles personal information. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). If we offer the Service in a way that brings it under other privacy laws, we will update this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information How we get it Why
Email address You enter it when you sign up or sign in To identify your account and send sign-in codes
Goals, to-dos, routines and completion records You enter them in the App To store your data and sync it between your devices
Time zone and first day of the week Time zone is read from your device’s settings; first day of the week is chosen by you To calculate dates correctly and display the calendar
Social profile: nickname, ID, bio and optional photo You set up and edit your profile To identify you to other users and help them find you
Invite token, follow and block relationships, reactions The App creates an invite token; you choose who to follow or block and what to like To share invites, apply visibility and blocks, and show likes
Notifications and notification settings The Service records relevant activity and saves your choices To show notifications in the App and respect your preferences
iOS push token, device language, time zone and app version The App registers your device when push notifications are enabled To deliver notifications to your device in the right language and respect quiet hours
Reports, including the category, details you provide and the reported content or account You submit a report in the App To review safety concerns and enforce the Community Rules

The App does not ask for your legal name, phone number, location or contacts. If you choose a profile photo, the App crops and re-encodes it on your device before uploading it, removing metadata such as EXIF location. Regular users sign in without a password, using a one-time code sent to their email.

Like most online services, our server provider (Supabase) automatically keeps technical logs of requests to our server. These logs may include your IP address, device and browser information (user agent) and the time of each request. We use them only to operate and secure the Service. These logs are kept for a limited period, currently up to 7 days.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To store what you enter and sync it across devices signed in to the same account
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To deliver social features, enforce visibility choices and review reports

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing or profiling.

Other users can see your social profile unless access is restricted. You can turn off ID search and share an invite link instead. Only you can see goals set to Only me. Followers can see goals set to Followers. Goals set to Mutual follows are only visible to people who follow you and whom you follow back. The same visibility applies to the goal’s to-dos, routines and completion records. When you like a completed item, the owner can see the reaction.

A block in either direction hides profiles and shared activity from each other. If social access is suspended, we hide the suspended account’s profile and shared activity from other users. We may also hide a photo or bio while reviewing reports.

3. Consent and withdrawing consent

By creating an account and using the App, you consent to us collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for the purposes described in this policy. We will ask for your We will tell you before we use your information for a new purpose, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. The social features are optional: we process social information only after you create a social profile.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account in the App (Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제)) or by contacting us. Because the App cannot sync your data without this information, withdrawing consent means you will no longer be able to use the Service.

4. Tracking and advertising

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not combine your information with data collected from other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not track you.

5. Retention and deletion

  • We generally keep account information for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account in the App (Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제)), we delete your account, goals, to-dos, routines, completion records and associated social data, including your profile photo. Report records, identifier holds and backup copies are subject to the retention details below. Profile photos are deleted from file storage at the same time. Our service providers may keep their own operational logs for a limited period under their policies.
  • When you delete a single goal, to-do or routine, it disappears from the App right away but is kept on our server, marked as deleted, until you delete your account. This lets the deletion sync to your other devices and be undone.
  • When you change your ID or delete your account, the old ID is held for 30 days so it cannot be claimed immediately by someone else.
  • Report records are deleted from our database when either the reporting or the reported account is deleted. Report alert emails in our support mailbox are deleted within 90 days after the report is resolved.
  • Database backups: we keep encrypted daily backups for up to 7 days to recover from failures. Deleted information disappears from backups within 7 days.
  • If a law requires us to keep certain information for longer, we will keep only that information and only for as long as the law requires.

Sign-in information and cached data stored on your device are removed from that device when you sign out or delete your account.

On iOS, sign-in tokens are stored in the device’s Keychain, and the last loaded records are cached in local app storage for offline viewing. Offline changes are not supported. Theme and gesture-guide preferences are stored on the device, separately from account data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete your content in the App at any time.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the App’s settings.
  • Push notifications are optional and can be turned off in your device’s settings.
  • To request access to or correction of your personal information, or to raise a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. To protect your account, please write from the email address you use to sign in. We will respond within 30 days and at no cost to you. If we need more time, as the law allows in limited cases, we will tell you why and when to expect our response.

8. Security

  • All communication between the App and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Database access controls enforce goal visibility, blocks and social suspensions.
  • On macOS, the App runs inside the App Sandbox.
  • On iOS, the App connects to the same service and does not request access to your camera, location or contacts. You can choose a profile photo through the system photo picker.

9. Children

The App is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. If you choose a language with the language switch, that choice is saved only in your browser’s local storage and is never sent to us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

If a change is material, such as using your information for a new purpose or sharing it with a new type of service provider, we will post it at least 30 days before it takes effect and email the address on your account.
