Support

Have a question or running into a problem? Send us an email.

Email support support@nodii.app

Including your device (iPhone or Mac), its iOS or macOS version and the App version (Settings → Version; on Mac, also Nodii → About Nodii in the menu bar) helps us look into it faster. Never send us a sign-in code or password.

Frequently asked questions

I didn’t get a sign-in code
  • Check your spam or promotions folder. Codes are sent from no-reply@mail.nodii.app.
  • Make sure the email address you entered has no typos.
  • If it still hasn’t arrived, press Resend Code on the sign-in screen. You can request a new code every 60 seconds. Each code works for 10 minutes.
Can I sync Nodii between my iPhone and Mac?

Yes. Sign in with the same email address on each device and your goals, todos and routines sync automatically. While you’re offline you can view your saved data but not change it; it updates once you’re back online.

What can I do offline?

You can view the records last loaded on that device. Adding, completing, moving and deleting need an internet connection, and the App shows “You’re offline, so you can only view your data.” If no records have been saved on the device yet, connect once to load them.

How do I change the App’s language?

Open Settings → Language and choose System, 한국어 or English. The setting is saved on each device, so your iPhone and Mac can use different languages.

How do swiping and long press work on iPhone?

Swipe a todo right to mark it as done, or left to move or delete it. Touch and hold a row to open its menu; the row’s menu and VoiceOver actions work without swiping too. On Mac, right-click a todo or use its ⋯ button.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, open Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. Your account and all goals, todos and routines are deleted together, and this can’t be undone.

The App says an update is required

Your current version can no longer be used with the service. Press Update in App Store on that screen to update Nodii. Your data stays as it is.
