Frequently asked questions

I didn’t get a sign-in code
  • Check your spam or promotions folder. Codes are sent from no-reply@mail.nodii.app.
  • Make sure the email address you entered has no typos.
  • If it still hasn’t arrived, press the resend button (코드 다시 받기) on the sign-in screen. You can request a new code every 60 seconds.
Can I use Nodii on more than one Mac?

Yes. Sign in with the same email address on each Mac and your goals, to-dos and routines sync automatically. While you’re offline you can view your saved data but not change it; it updates once you’re back online.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, open Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제) and follow the confirmation steps. Your account and all goals, to-dos and routines are deleted together, and this can’t be undone.

The App says a new version is required

Your current version can no longer be used with the service. Press the App Store에서 업데이트 (Update in App Store) button on that screen to update Nodii. Your data stays as it is.
