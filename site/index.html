Make room for every goal

Nodii gathers your todos and routines by goal. Check off your day and watch your calendar fill with color, on iPhone and Mac.

Free · iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later) · Mac (macOS 12 or later)

How it works

A small list that grows with you

Every todo you finish leaves a mark in its goal’s color. Over a month, your calendar shows where your days went.

Goals

A day organized by goal

Give each goal a name and a color. Each day’s todos sit under their goal, and checking one off fills it with that color.

  • Drag to reorder todos within a goal
  • Move a todo to tomorrow or any date you choose
  • Archive a finished goal and keep its past records
Study
  • Learn 30 Spanish words
  • Solve 2 coding problems
  • Review chapter 3 notes
Workout
  • Run 5 km

Go ahead, check them off.

Calendar

Watch your month fill with color

Each date gets a sprout stamp. It takes on the colors of the goals you finished that day, and it sprouts leaves once everything is done.

  • See how many todos are left on every date
  • Jump to any day from the monthly calendar
  • Shrink the Mac window for a compact week view

Routines

Set it once with routines

Repeat daily, on chosen weekdays, or on chosen dates each month. Routines appear on the right days by themselves.

  • Choose an interval, like every 2 weeks or every 3 days
  • Skip just one busy day
  • Stop as of today and keep your past records

Change plans without worry

  • Move or delete a day’s unfinished todos all at once
  • Swipe on iPhone; right-click or ⋯ on Mac

iPhone + Mac

One list on your iPhone and Mac

Sign in with the same email on each device. Your goals, todos and routines stay in sync.

  • Syncs right away

    Check something off on your iPhone and it’s already done on your Mac.

  • Readable offline

    Lost your connection? Your saved records are still there to read.

  • No passwords

    Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email. New here? That signs you up too.

Free. No ads, analytics or tracking. Delete your account and all its data anytime in Settings.
