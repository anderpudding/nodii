Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026 · Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Sungjun Lee (“we”, “us”), the developer of the Mac app Nodii (the “App”) and its related online service (together, the “Service”), handles personal information. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Information we collect

Information How we get it Why
Email address You enter it when you sign up or sign in To identify your account and send sign-in codes
Goals, to-dos, routines and completion records You enter them in the App To store your data and sync it between your Macs
Time zone and first day of the week Time zone is read from your Mac’s settings; first day of the week is chosen by you To calculate dates correctly and display the calendar

The App does not ask for your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other information not listed above. Regular users sign in without a password, using a one-time code sent to their email.

Like most online services, our server provider (Supabase) automatically keeps technical logs of requests to our server. These logs may include your IP address, device and browser information (user agent) and the time of each request. We use them only to operate and secure the Service, and on our current plan they are deleted automatically after 1 day.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To store what you enter and sync it across Macs signed in to the same account
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing or profiling.

3. Consent and withdrawing consent

By creating an account and using the App, you consent to us collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for the purposes described in this policy. We will ask for your consent again before using your information for any new purpose.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account in the App (Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제)) or by contacting us. Because the App cannot sync your data without this information, withdrawing consent means you will no longer be able to use the Service.

4. Tracking and advertising

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not combine your information with data collected from other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not track you.

5. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account in the App (Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제)), your account and all goals, to-dos, routines and completion records are deleted from our server.
  • When you delete a single goal, to-do or routine, it disappears from the App right away but is kept on our server, marked as deleted, until you delete your account. This lets the deletion sync to your other Macs and be undone.
  • We do not currently keep separate database backups, so data deleted with your account cannot be restored. If we start keeping backups, we will update this policy with how long backup copies are kept.
  • If a law requires us to keep certain information for longer, we will keep only that information and only for as long as the law requires.

Sign-in information and cached data stored on your Mac are removed from that Mac when you sign out or delete your account.

6. Service providers and storage outside Canada

We use the following service providers to run the App.

Provider Purpose Information processed
Supabase Account authentication, database storage and sync (database hosted in the AWS us-west-1 region, Northern California, United States) Email address, content you enter, time zone and first day of the week
Resend Sending sign-in code emails Email address

Because these providers store and process your information in the United States, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. We rely on these providers’ contractual commitments to protect your information, and they may use it only to provide their services to us.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete your content in the App at any time.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the App’s settings.
  • To request access to or correction of your personal information, or to raise a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. To protect your account, please write from the email address you use to sign in. We will respond within 30 days and at no cost to you. If we need more time, as the law allows in limited cases, we will tell you why and when to expect our response.

8. Security

  • All communication between the App and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Row-level access control in the database lets each user access only their own data.
  • The App runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and uses only the permission to make outgoing network connections.

9. Children

The App is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

11. Contact

  • Privacy contact: Sungjun Lee
  • Email: support@nodii.app

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, where British Columbia’s PIPA applies, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the changes and the new effective date on this page. If a change is material, such as using your information for a new purpose or sharing it with a new type of service provider, we will post it at least 30 days before it takes effect and email the address on your account. Where the law requires your consent to a change, we will ask for it before the change applies to you.
