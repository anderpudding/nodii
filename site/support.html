Support

Have a question or running into a problem? Send us an email.

Contact: support@nodii.app

Including your macOS or iOS version and app version (Settings / 설정 → 버전; on Mac, also Nodii → Nodii 정보 in the menu bar) helps us look into it faster. Never send us a sign-in code or password.

The App’s interface is currently in Korean.

Frequently asked questions

I didn’t get a sign-in code
  • Check your spam or promotions folder. Codes are sent from no-reply@mail.nodii.app.
  • Make sure the email address you entered has no typos.
  • If it still hasn’t arrived, press the resend button (코드 다시 받기) on the sign-in screen. You can request a new code every 60 seconds.
Can I sync Nodii between my Mac and iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with the same email address on each device and your goals, to-dos and routines sync automatically. While you’re offline you can view your saved data but not change it; it updates once you’re back online.

What can I do offline on iPhone?

You can view the records last loaded on that device. Adding, completing, moving and deleting require an internet connection. If no records have been cached yet, connect once to load them. The App shows “오프라인이라 보기만 할 수 있어요” while offline.

How do swiping and long press work on iPhone?

Swipe a to-do right to complete it, or left to reveal Move (옮기기) and Delete (삭제). Long press a row to open its menu; the row’s menu button and VoiceOver actions also work without swiping. Routine rows offer completion, skipping and stopping in their menu. The first sign-in guide can be skipped without losing any actions.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, open Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제) and follow the confirmation steps. Your account and all goals, to-dos and routines are deleted together, and this can’t be undone.

The App says a new version is required (“새 버전이 필요해요”)

Your current version can no longer be used with the service. Press the App Store에서 업데이트 (Update in App Store) button on that screen to update Nodii. Your data stays as it is.
